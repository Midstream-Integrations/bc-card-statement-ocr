# Privacy

This extension runs inside your Business Central tenant and stores its data in your Business Central database.

It sends card statement PDFs, the selected cardholder, invoice numbers, vendor details and approver names and emails to the **OCR Host you configure**, and that server calls back into Business Central through the API pages in this app. Where that data is stored and for how long is set by whoever runs the OCR server. Emails are sent through your own Business Central email accounts.

Midstream Integrations does not collect telemetry or any data from this extension.
